The ribbon · one element, three jobs
Bookmark
Closed: it hangs out of the top like a ribbon out of a shut book. Tap it to pick up where you left off.
Waveform
Recording: the ribbon ripples with your voice.
Playhead
Listening: it lies under the word being spoken.
The one idea
Speech-to-text and text-to-speech are the same object seen from two sides: text with time on every word. Tap a word, and the sound goes there. Both directions, one gesture.
Why paper
Reviewers of iPhone Duo say the inner screen looks like real paper. Verso leans into it: a matte paper ground, ink type, no chrome. The red ribbon is the only saturated thing on screen, so your eye always knows where it is.
