The ice had been quiet all night, and by morning the wind had dropped to nothing. We ate in the tent with the flap open, which we had not done in nine days.

Estraven checked the sledge lashings twice. I checked them a third time, which he allowed without comment. That was the shape our friendship had taken: a set of small permissions.

By the time we started the light was flat and shadowless, the kind that makes distance a guess.
